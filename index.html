⟶Computer Scinece Engineering

Computer Science Engineering (CSE) is a broad field that combines computer science principles with engineering practices to design, develop, and implement computer systems and software. It involves studying both the theoretical aspects of computing and the practical application of these theories to real-world problems. CSE focuses on areas like algorithms, software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and system architectures.

⟶Data Science

Data science is an interdisciplinary academic field [1] that uses statistics, scientific computing, scientific methods, processing, scientific visualization, algorithms and systems to extract or extrapolate knowledge from potentially noisy, structured, or unstructured data. [2]Data science also integrates domain knowledge from the underlying application domain (e.g., natural sciences, information technology, and medicine). [3] Data science is multifaceted and can be described as a science, a research paradigm, a research method , a discipline, a workflow, and a profession.
